# FoxGlove

The reference character: her manifest, personality and director are here, and her body and face are a profile in the player (`player/assets/characters/foxglove.hzt`, with her model and rig in `player/assets/foxglove/`). This repo has no animations for her, so she holds her rest pose; her face, emotions, lip sync and hair and cloth physics work.

Files: `character.yaml`, `system.md`, `director.yaml`.
