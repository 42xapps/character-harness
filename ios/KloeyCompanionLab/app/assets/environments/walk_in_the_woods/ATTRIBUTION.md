# Walk in the Woods

[Walk in the Woods](https://poly.pizza/m/38m6Q1H12DU) by [Don Carson](https://poly.pizza/u/Don%20Carson), licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), via Poly Pizza.

Downloaded on 2026-10-18. For the Kloey Companion Lab adaptation, one foreground boulder was removed, the remaining static meshes were combined, and the scene was scaled and positioned around the character. The unmodified download is preserved under `art/forest/WalkInTheWoods.source.glb`. `tools/prepare-forest.py` reproduces the Blender export.

The app displays the author, source link, license link and adaptation notice under Your view > Forest credits. This asset retains its own CC BY 3.0 license, separately from the Apache 2.0 character harness code.
